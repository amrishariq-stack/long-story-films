About

A filmmaker who listens first.

Long Story Films is the work of Mas — a filmmaker who’s been telling wedding stories since long before this studio existed.

Before relocating to Northern Virginia, Mas spent years filming weddings across cultures — capturing the kind of family moments, quiet emotions, and meaningful traditions that most cameras miss.

Long Story Films is the continuation of that work. Built here, for couples whose weddings are part of a bigger story — a meeting, a courtship, a family, a culture, a future.

Every couple is interviewed before their wedding. Every film is structured like a short documentary. Every story is told from the beginning.

The Long Story Approach

How Long Story Films works

Three principles that shape every wedding film we make.

01

We start before the wedding day.

Every couple gets a pre-wedding story session — a real conversation about how you met, what built your bond, and the family who shaped you. Your voices become the spine of your film.

02

We film like documentary, not music video.

Every film has a clear beginning, middle, and end. Family is captured deliberately. Quiet moments matter as much as big ones. The story is built first; the music supports it, not the other way around.

03

We edit for permanence.

No trending sounds. No gimmicks that age out. Your film is built to feel right in five years, fifteen years, and forty. It’s a film for your grandchildren, not just your Instagram.

The Process

The Long Story process

From your first inquiry to the moment you watch your film — here’s how we work.

01

Inquire

You reach out through our contact form. We send back our pricing guide and a few questions about your wedding within 24 hours.

02

Discovery call

A 30-minute conversation — usually over Zoom. We hear about your wedding, your families, and your story. You hear about our process. No pressure, no sales pitch. If we’re a fit, we move forward.

03

Book + story session

We send a contract and a small retainer secures your date. A few weeks before the wedding, we sit down with you for a 45–90 minute story session — a real conversation that becomes the foundation of your film.

04

The wedding

We arrive early, work calmly, capture the day with intention, and stay out of the way. You won’t notice us much — that’s the point.

05

The film

Within 8–14 weeks, you receive your wedding film, your ceremony edit, and your social cuts. One round of revisions is included. After that, the film is yours forever.
